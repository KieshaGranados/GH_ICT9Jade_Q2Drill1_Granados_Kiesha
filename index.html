🌕 Beginner's Guide to Pickleball



What is Pickleball?

Pickleball is a paddle sport blending tennis, badminton, and table tennis. It can be played indoors or outdoors.


📒 Basic Rules

  • Serve diagonally
  • Double bounce rule
  • No volley zone
  • Score

🥅 Pickleball Equipment

  • Paddle
  • Plastic ball
  • Net
  • Court
